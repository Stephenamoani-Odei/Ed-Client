# EdenPlus Client Site

The public registration and payment page for **EdenPlus Education Consult**'s current program.

## About

This is where prospective clients register for EdenPlus programs and confirm their payment — a simple, guided experience from sign-up to confirmation, with no account or app download required.

## Who it's for

Built for anyone registering for an EdenPlus Education Consult program, whether they're a first-time visitor or returning to complete a payment.

## Features

- **Simple registration** — name, contact details, and location, in one short form
- **Returning visitor recognition** — if you've registered before, the site finds your existing record instead of starting over
- **Flexible payment** — pay in full or in part, whatever suits you
- **Guided payment confirmation** — clear instructions for completing payment, with confirmation once it's verified
- **Instant ticket** — once your payment is confirmed, you receive your ticket number by SMS
- **Privacy Policy** — clearly available on every screen, explaining exactly what's collected and how it's used

## How it works

Register, choose your payment amount, complete payment following the instructions provided, and submit your confirmation details. An EdenPlus admin verifies it, and you're notified the moment it's approved — along with your ticket number.

## Security

Only the information needed to register and confirm payment is collected. Personal details are never sold or shared, and are only used to run the program you're registering for. See the in-app Privacy Policy for full details.

## Status

Actively maintained and in use for current EdenPlus Education Consult programs.

## Support

For help with registration or payment, contact EdenPlus Education Consult directly.
